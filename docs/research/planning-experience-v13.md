# Revision 13: broader candidate proposals

15 October 2026. The revision-13 proposal engineering milestone is implemented and
its frozen comparison meets the registered structural targets. Final hint-amendment
replay, controlled timing, evidence reconciliation and the requested assistant play
review are complete. This closes the local revision-13 handoff with the performance
and quality limitations below. Human experience has not been measured.

This is an explicit revision of the existing energy-and-switch planning activity.
Revision 12 remains frozen, including its original failed decision-sequence
diversity target. The revision-13 comparison uses fresh registered cases; its
baseline counts do not replace the earlier revision-12 results.

## Contract

New proposal behavior preserves the physical and strategy gates, deterministic
saved replay, at least two meaningful decisions and a witnessed dependency on every
shortest route. Candidate selection retains revision 12's quality bounds: no extra
dead-end choices or forced steps relative to the eligible pool's strongest candidate,
and at most one extra rule row or action. The budget remains 48 candidate evaluations.

The generator starts from a development-derived bank of 257 physical foundations
in 47 families, covering 29 global label-invariant role sequences. It assesses
independent starts and physical edits drawn from a population balanced across
action roles. Final selection compensates for role sequences that occur in several
level/profile groups using a static, inverse-frequency prior. The draw, candidate
quality and supporting physical rules can be independently replayed. No request
depends on previous requests or precomputed answers.

Relabeling or adding story text is not counted as structural improvement. The
development records retain unsuccessful experiments. Unknown causal coverage
cannot pass. The bank is finite and the physical rules are unchanged; this is not
unrestricted mechanic invention. Offline mining cost is outside the per-request
48-candidate budget and timing comparison.

The [registered protocol](../../artifacts/engine-capability-v13/2026-10-15/protocol.json)
fixed fresh matched/heldout cases and comparison gates before implementation freeze.
The targets required at least two additional canonical role sequences unless at the
20-case ceiling, at least 16 distinct sequences overall, retained physical variety,
multiple candidate roles on at least half of the final frontiers, and no increase
in mean dead-end choices or forced steps.

Eighteen of 20 final candidate frontiers contain multiple independently derived
invariant role sequences, exceeding the target of ten. All 16 heldout cases pass
the independent physical/profile, certificate, counterfactual, public-state/hint
and every-shortest-route causal-link checks. All 20 selection audits pass; they
reconstruct 485 recorded candidates representing 472 distinct physical models.
All 17 format/batch requests pass, covering 86 items.

Historical replay preserves 2,669 distinct revision 1–12 bundle outcomes: 2,560
accepted bundles containing 2,851 items and 109 exact prior rejections. Another
108 revision-12 raw models retain their exact certificate and scenario outputs.
The frozen release checks pass [2,408 creator tests](../../artifacts/engine-capability-v13/2026-10-15/release/creator-final.json)
and [six browser checks](../../artifacts/engine-capability-v13/2026-10-15/release/browser-final.json),
including episode replay for revisions 11, 12 and 13. These release results precede
the final hint-only amendment. Its 155 focused tests pass, along with replay of 56
frozen models (5,083 states and 15,249 hints), a 40-panel amended gallery check and
the final episode DOM retry. The initial final-hint DOM invocation's relative
dependency-path failure is retained. Current source matches the declared amendment; the complete
creator suite was not rerun after that isolated change.

Controlled warmed generation medians are 0.167s for revision 12 and 0.274s for
revision 13: a 64.2% increase (+107ms), exceeding the registered 1.5× warning.
Speed improvement was not mandatory. Development-only profiling finds fewer
duplicate proposals and more graph/quality evaluations within the same nominal
budget. No small proven output-preserving fix emerged, so the frozen generator
and the substantial latency regression are retained. [Performance review](../../artifacts/engine-capability-v13/2026-10-15/release/performance-review.md).

## Compatibility and player experience

New requests use engine revision 13 by default.
Saved revisions 1–12 preserve their existing proposal and replay behavior. Concise
revision-12 stories, action-aware dialogue and grounded hints remain the presentation
contract; there is no new activity-menu entry or additional mechanical rule.

The increase in complete role sequences does not establish greater variety of
meaningful decisions: that measure remains 14/20. Canonical route choices can also
depend on label-based action tie breaking, which is why the invariant measure is
reported separately. Shortcut resistance did not improve. Uniform-tie horizon-2
policy success rises from 0% to 39.375%, and horizon-3 success rises from 22.917% to
59.531%; these shallow policies solve more candidate puzzles. General difficulty
improvement must not be claimed.

Human participant count is zero. The user-requested [assistant play review](../../artifacts/engine-capability-v13/2026-10-15/release/assistant-play-review.md)
covers the baseline and completed revision-13 easy/medium/hard episodes, with prior
source knowledge. The final UI review verifies the charger hint, recharge undo,
dead-end recovery and successful zero-energy arrival. The write-up records both
coherent character callbacks and repetitive stories/map-reading burden. This
completes the self-review requested by the user. A blinded participant study and
claims about enjoyment, perceived difficulty or desire to play again remain
separate future work.

The [final report](../../artifacts/engine-capability-v13/2026-10-15/independent-validation.md)
records all 18 passed engineering gates and the latency warning. The
[earlier progress record](../../artifacts/engine-capability-v13/2026-10-15/independent-validation-pending.md),
original freeze, raw evidence and original gallery remain intact. The
[current paired gallery](../../artifacts/engine-capability-v13/2026-10-15/amendment/sample-gallery.html)
uses the validated hint amendment.
