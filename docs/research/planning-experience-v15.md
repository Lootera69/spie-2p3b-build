# Revision 15: compact audit evidence and selective policy scoring

The revision-14 repair has three concrete changes. Candidate comparisons retain
their raw physical rules, attempt metadata and a SHA-256 commitment to the full
derived record. `expand_comparisons` reconstructs and checks every measurement;
`audit_selection` replays the final lottery. Final-model certification remains
independent of search provenance. Neither a digest nor a saved selection claim
substitutes for physical/causal checks.

Selection retains the existing cost bounds and per-signature dominance pruning.
It draws a meaningful role before applying the level's depth preference within
that role, then draws a decision signature. Only those candidates need the exact
horizon-2/5 policy evaluation. Cached foundation analyses are treated as immutable;
returned physical and compact evidence records remain isolated from caller edits.

The quiz uses numbered room references to state the complete rules concisely.
The full story, named map, consequences, hints and arrival-success objective remain
in the playable episode. This wording is versioned so saved revisions 1–14 replay
exactly. The 2 MiB API body cap is unchanged.

Fresh evaluation was registered before implementation, and 141 source files were
frozen before fixed outcomes. On 20 matched cases, roles remain 15 (target 17),
dependency signatures remain 17 and physical structures remain 20. All 24 heldout
cases pass. Mean rule rows remain 10.7, traps/forced actions remain zero, and mean
whitespace-counted stem words fall 120.2 → 63.9. Studio compliance uses its stricter
alphanumeric regex, separately checked across all 17 format/batch requests.

Controlled median generation falls 0.160435 → 0.126762 seconds, a 20.99% reduction.
All 120 timed model hashes match frozen cases. Maximum-history payloads peak at
1,132,215 bytes. All 86 format items, 20 independently reconstructed selection
pools (462 distinct raw models), 2,720 historical bundle outcomes and 386 raw
certificate/scenario records pass. Earlier exact rejections remain rejections.

The 120-case development sample improved role/signature/structure variety, but
that gain did not repeat in the matched evaluation. The candidate therefore meets
21/22 engineering/release checks and remains Experimental. No claim is made that
human enjoyment or difficulty improved; there were zero human participants.
Future selection work needs fresh evidence and must preserve this failed target.
