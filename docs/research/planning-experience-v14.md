# Revision 14 decision judgment — evaluated local candidate

This iteration evaluates consequential decisions and the planning horizon needed
to complete a puzzle. Every legal goal arrival counts as success; shortest-route
efficiency is an optional challenge. It preserves saved revisions 1–13.

The generator keeps the 48-assessment budget and existing physical foundations.
It compresses every shortest route to meaningful decisions, records viable
alternatives and ordinal completion costs, and compares the directed dependencies
between those decisions. The signature ignores room labels and forced travel.
Depth preference is capped at two decisions for easy/medium and three for hard.
A three-decision chain on every hard route remains a diagnostic, not a guarantee.

Selection first limits traps, forced steps, reading rules and extra actions
relative to its best recorded candidate. It then draws meaningful role sequences
and dependency signatures with inverse support across the twelve existing
development foundation families. This static prior supplies no acceptance proof.
Within the chosen form, level-specific policy scores prefer successful completion
and fewer dead ends, with efficient completion recorded separately. Exact rational
policy probabilities use integer masses with a common per-layer denominator.
The optimization preserved all twenty saved development models and matched the
independent raw-rule policy implementation.

[the candidate manifest](../../artifacts/engine-capability-v14/2026-10-15/candidate/freeze.json).

evaluation. Seed identifiers were exposed during implementation and recovery;
full seed blinding is not claimed. The immutable original attempt is retained
even where it fails. The final report distinguishes correctness from improvement.
