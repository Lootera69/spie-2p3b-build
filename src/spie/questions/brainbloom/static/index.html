What is a clue puzzle?

Imagine three cards: Tiger, Lion and Owl. Tiger comes before Lion. Owl is not next to Tiger. Which card is last?

Show the example reasoning

Tiger and Owl must occupy the two ends. Tiger cannot be last because it comes before Lion. So Tiger is first, Lion is second and Owl is last.

Supported rule patterns

Use the exact labels you defined. Examples:

  • Tiger is before Lion.
  • Owl cannot be next to Tiger.
  • Tiger is paired with Red.
  • Blue is not paired with Tea.
  • Tea is in position 1.
  • Lion is immediately before Owl.
  • Owl is between Tiger and Lion.
  • Red is 2 positions away from Blue.

“After”, “is next to” and “is not in position” also work. One to three groups; up to 20 rules.
